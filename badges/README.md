# Your own club crests

Put a crest here named after the club's Wikidata ID, e.g. `Q223566.png` for Al Ahly
(.png, .svg, .webp or .jpg). It replaces any crest from Wikidata or TheSportsDB after the next
"Update data" run.

Find a club's ID in `data/data.json` (criteria keys look like `club:Q223566`) or on wikidata.org.
Square images with a transparent background look best.
